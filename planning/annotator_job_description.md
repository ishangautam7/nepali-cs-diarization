# Annotator — Nepali–English speech research study

## Role

We are hiring **bilingual (Nepali–English) annotators** to label conversational
speech for an academic speech-research project. You will correct and transcribe
recordings of natural Nepali–English conversations in the ELAN annotation tool, and
do short listening checks on an existing speech corpus. Your labels are the study's
ground truth, so care and consistency are what the work is paid for — not speed.

## Requirements

- **Fluent in Nepali and English** (understand fast, casual, mixed Nepali–English
  conversation with technical content).
- **Fast and accurate typing in both Devanagari and Latin scripts** — you will switch
  scripts constantly within a sentence.
- **Good hearing** and the patience to replay audio closely.
- **Closed-back headphones** (own or provided — [specify]).
- A **stable internet connection.**
- Comfortable using a laptop/desktop and a modern web browser. **You do not need your
  own computer for the project data** — annotation runs on project-provided equipment
  (see Equipment below).
- Careful, consistent, follows written guidelines. Prior transcription/annotation or
  linguistics experience is a plus, not required.

## Tasks

- **Job A — conversations.** In ELAN, work a 2–3-person Nepali–English conversation in
  two passes. **Pass 1:** correct (or, for some sessions, type from scratch) the full
  transcript of the whole session — English in Latin script, Nepali in Devanagari —
  and fix who is speaking and when, with one full listen-through; every audio channel
  is linked so you can solo each speaker. **Pass 2:** tighten segment boundaries and
  speaker/transcript around a set of marker points. You also flag ambiguous loanwords
  for the team and tag backchannels and names.
- **Job B — listening checks (some annotators).** On a web page, judge short audio
  snippets: is there a real language switch at the marked point, is it the same
  speaker on both sides, and is the mark in the right place.
- Follow the project annotation guidelines exactly, and join a short weekly
  calibration meeting.
