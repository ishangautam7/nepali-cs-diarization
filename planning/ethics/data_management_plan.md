This plan covers the D2 conversation recordings. The university's own data-protection rules take precedence over anything here.

## Collection

- Recorded in [RECORDING LOCATION] with a multitrack recorder.
- Each participant wears a wired lavalier microphone; one room microphone records the whole room.
- A clap at the start and end marks the recording.
- Captured: audio only. No video. No names stored in the recording filenames — each session gets a code (e.g. `S03`).
- A separate, access-controlled key links session codes to participant names and consent forms. Stored apart from the audio.

## Transfer

- Audio is moved from the recorder to the project storage over an encrypted channel.
- No upload to personal cloud accounts or third-party services.

## Storage

- Raw audio encrypted at rest.
- Held on [STORAGE LOCATION — e.g. the project box], mirrored to [BACKUP LOCATION].
- Encryption keys held only by the research team.

## Access

- Research team: full access.
- Hired bilingual annotators: access to the audio they transcribe, under a signed confidentiality agreement, on [ANNOTATION ENVIRONMENT].
- Nobody else, unless a participant opted into a sharing tier.

## Retention

- Raw audio kept until [RETENTION DATE], then deleted.
- The name-to-code key is deleted at [KEY DELETION DATE — recommend no later than the raw-audio date].
- Derived, redacted data (transcripts, annotations, timestamps) may be kept longer per the release tiers below.

## De-identification

- Names and identifying details are bleeped (masked) in any released audio; voices stay recognisable.
- Transcripts and annotations are redacted to remove names and identifying details.
- No speaker embeddings (voiceprints) are ever released.

## Release (tiered consent)

A participant's data enters a tier only if they ticked that box. Because everyone's voice is on the room mic and, faintly, on the other lavaliers, audio from a session is shared or released at a tier only if everyone in that session chose that tier.

- Team only (default): no release.
- Data-use agreement: data shared with named researchers under signed terms; includes audio.
- Public audio: released only when everyone in the session opted in; names bleeped, but voices stay recognisable.
- Public transcripts/annotations only: released without audio when everyone in the session opted in; redacted.

## Deletion and withdrawal

- A participant may withdraw until the results are published (expected [PUBLICATION DATE]). Nothing is shared or released before then.
- On withdrawal, the whole session's audio is removed from the study, along with the derived data, because the session cannot be released without that person's voice.
- At project end, raw audio is deleted on [RETENTION DATE] regardless of withdrawal.

## Roles

- Data controller / PI: [PI NAME].
- Day-to-day data steward: [DATA STEWARD].
- Annotators: [NAMES/COUNT], under confidentiality agreement.

## Legal basis

Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) requires consent before collecting a person's personal or family data and limits use to the stated purpose ([Privacy Act 2075 overview](https://www.dataguidance.com/opinion/nepal-introduction-individual-privacy-act-2018)). Processing here relies on explicit written consent and purpose-limited use, with the tiered consent above governing any sharing. [UNIVERSITY] rules and the conditions of [APPROVING BODY] take precedence.
