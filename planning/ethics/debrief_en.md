## The detail we held back

Before the session, we told you we study how well automatic speech technology tells speakers apart in Nepali–English conversation. That was true. We held back one more specific point.

Our specific question is whether switching between Nepali and English within a conversation makes the technology mistakenly think the speaker has changed — in other words, whether code-switching confuses "who spoke when".

We did not tell you this in advance because, if you knew we were interested in language switching, you might have switched more or less than you naturally would, and that would spoil the recording. We did not give you any false information. We only held back this one detail until now.
