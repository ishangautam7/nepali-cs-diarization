# Ethics statement

> **Template, not in use.** This is a template for the planned D2-R conversation recordings, which were never made. No ethics approval was sought or obtained for them. Nothing in this file applies to the study reported in the paper (see `README.md` in this folder).

The study was approved by [APPROVING BODY], reference [APPROVAL REFERENCE]. All conversation participants gave written informed consent before recording. The specific hypothesis — that code-switching may trigger false speaker changes — was withheld beforehand, as it could alter how people speak; participants received a written debrief disclosing it and gave written re-consent afterwards. No false information was given. Participants were paid per session regardless of withdrawal. Audio is released only with consent and only at a tier chosen by everyone in a session; if anyone withdraws, the whole session's audio is removed. Released audio has names bleeped but voices remain recognisable, and no voiceprints (speaker embeddings) are ever released. The public ASR corpus (D1) is CC BY-NC; long-form audio of its source videos was obtained from YouTube for private, non-commercial analysis only and is not redistributed, only video IDs and timestamps.
