# Recruitment notice

## Volunteers wanted: a study on speech technology and bilingual conversation

We are looking for people who speak both Nepali and English to take part in a short research study at [UNIVERSITY].

### What is it?

We study how well automatic speech technology can tell speakers apart in natural Nepali–English conversation. You and one or two other people simply have a relaxed conversation about everyday topics while we record it.

### What's involved?

- One session of about 45–60 minutes.
- A friendly, unscripted conversation with 1–2 other bilingual speakers.
- You wear a small clip-on microphone; the room is also recorded.
- Paid [AMOUNT] for taking part.

### Who can take part?

- Adults (18 or older).
- Comfortable speaking both Nepali and English.

### Taking part is voluntary

You can stop at any time and withdraw afterwards without giving a reason. Full details, including how your recording is stored and your privacy protected, are in the information sheet we give you before you decide.
